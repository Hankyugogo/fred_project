Brief Commentary
Last night was not a panic tape; it was a caution reset. As oil and yields rose together, investors reduced speed in risk-taking.

The key distinction is “more caution,” not “full risk-off.” That is why some sectors still held firm while indices drifted lower.

In this regime, reading flow rotation is more useful than forcing a single index view. Energy strength plus tech resilience is a classic rotation signal.

For Korean investors, confirmation-based entries remain preferable to open-gap chasing. Position sizing should follow alignment in oil, yields, and FX.

Overnight Lead
The practical story overnight was a shift from comfort to caution. With oil and U.S. 10Y yields both moving up, markets trimmed risk and major indices closed modestly lower. At the same time, tech and semis did not fully break, suggesting caution rather than panic. Today’s tone will likely depend on whether oil/yields keep rising or stabilize again.
