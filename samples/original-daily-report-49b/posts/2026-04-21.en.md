Short commentary
Overnight was not about a big index move. It was about risk appetite cooling at the margin, visible through a larger VIX move.

In plain terms, investors chose confirmation over aggression. No fresh official execution signal on major geopolitical routes meant less willingness to add broad risk.

Sector divergence also mattered more than the headline index print. Financials and industrials held up, while consumer and healthcare lagged.

For Korea today, the practical sequence is simple: confirm what happened, understand why markets reacted that way, then track USD/KRW, yields, and foreign flow before adding size.
