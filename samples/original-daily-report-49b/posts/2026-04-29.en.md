The key overnight message was calm pricing despite noisy headlines. That left indices in a narrow range while rates and the dollar capped upside in risk assets. A sudden shift in official wording can still reprice oil and volatility first.

Short commentary
In this regime, confirmed action matters more than loud wording. Comments can shake intraday prices, but without formal execution they often fail to sustain index trends. Speed of confirmation is now more important than speed of headlines.

The transmission sequence is still clear. Event shock first hits oil and volatility, then rates, then index and sector pricing. If you track this order, intraday decisions become simpler.

Today the core variable set is rates plus the dollar. If yields ease, growth can stabilize; if yields and USD/KRW rise together, Korea growth volatility can widen quickly. First-hour flow confirmation remains more important than opening-gap emotion.
