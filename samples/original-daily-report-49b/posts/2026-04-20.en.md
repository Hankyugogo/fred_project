Brief Commentary
The key point is not that risk is gone, but that markets temporarily repriced risk premium lower. Oil and VIX stabilized first, then equities followed. That sequence explains the move better than any single headline.

At the same time, the gap between comments and formal execution is still open. The wider that gap stays, the larger the intraday reversal risk becomes. Confirmation-based positioning remains more robust than directional conviction.

Rates also did not collapse. Equity optimism and bond caution are coexisting, so sector selection matters more than index direction in this phase. Even in a positive tape, realized outcomes can diverge sharply.

For Korean investors, confirmation in the first 1–2 hours is still more effective than open chasing. Position size should increase only when oil, FX, and foreign-flow signals align.
