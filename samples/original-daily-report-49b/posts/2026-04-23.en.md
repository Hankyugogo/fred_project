5) Short commentary (3-4 paragraphs)
The key overnight message is not just that indices rose, but why they rose. Yields stayed contained and VIX moved lower, so risk appetite recovered in an orderly way. The stronger Nasdaq move changed market tone the most.

At the same time, oil is still at a high enough level to matter. It is not a panic trigger now, but a few more up days could quickly revive inflation concern. That would feed back into yields and pressure growth valuations again.

Geopolitical risk is still a live variable, but the market is now focusing more on execution than words. In plain terms, official sanctions or transport restrictions matter more than loud comments. If that line is blurred, intraday reversals can become frequent.

For Korea today, avoid forcing a one-way call too early. The first 1-2 hours of USD/KRW, foreign futures flow, and semiconductor breadth should guide positioning more than the opening print.
